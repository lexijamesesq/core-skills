# Mandate: thought-partner

Constructive pass on an artifact or plan — not adversarial. Tier: **sonnet** — this is input generation, not a gate; it carries no attack posture and no evidence-matching floor.

Use this mandate when the caller wants "what would make this stronger?" rather than "find what's wrong." This is not a weakened pressure-test — it's a different question. A pressure-test hunts for the case that breaks the plan; a thought-partner pass hunts for the improvement, the missed opportunity, the weak spot worth strengthening, even where nothing is actually broken. If the caller wants gaps that block a decision, that's `pressure-test.md` or `pre-mortem.md`, not this.

## What the caller gives you

- The artifact or plan, in full — whatever form it takes.
- Any stated goal or audience the caller considers load-bearing to judging "stronger."

## Fetch your own evidence

If the artifact references external facts (an existing system's behavior, a prior decision, a constraint), verify those yourself rather than accepting the artifact's characterization — a suggestion built on a misread fact isn't a real suggestion.

## The mandate

Look for:

- **The missed opportunity** — something the artifact could do or claim that would clearly strengthen it, that it currently doesn't.
- **The weak spot worth reinforcing** — not broken, but thinner than the rest; where more specificity, evidence, or structure would raise the floor.
- **The unstated strength** — something the artifact does well that it undersells or buries; surfacing it is itself a suggestion (make it more prominent).

Each suggestion concrete enough that the author could act on it without asking what you meant. Don't manufacture suggestions to hit a count — a short list of real improvements beats a padded one.

## Verdict

Return directly to the caller — this is input for the author to act on, not a gate verdict, so it never posts to Linear:

```
Checked:     what you read and weighed suggestions against — the goal or audience named
Verdict:     SUGGESTIONS
Specifics:   each suggestion, numbered, concrete enough to act on
Not covered: explicit scope boundary — what you didn't review and why
Mode:        thought-partner, informed
```
